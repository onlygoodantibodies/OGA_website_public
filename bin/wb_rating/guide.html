Rating a knockout western blot

Compare the wild-type lane (WT) with the knockout lane (KO). Work down the five questions in order and stop at the first one that gives a result. The blots below are drawings made to show each case; the red arrow marks the band the knockout removes or reduces.

Knockdowns. Some protein always remains after a knockdown, so a residual band at the target's size is expected and never counts against the antibody. Judge the other bands as usual.
Size is secondary. A band the knockout removes is the target, whatever its apparent molecular weight.
Ignore the dye front at the bottom of the gel, and any band that appears only in the KO lane, including a shortened form of the target.
