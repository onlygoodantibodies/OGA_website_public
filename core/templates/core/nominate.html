What happens next

  • Your request is recorded and counted. How many people have asked for a gene, and which applications they need, is one of the things we weigh when deciding what to run next.
  • We cannot promise to take it on. Characterising one gene properly means knockout cell lines, every commercial antibody for it, and four applications' worth of bench work — so what gets done is limited by funding and capacity, not by interest.
  • If you have a funding route, tell us. That is the thing most likely to turn a nomination into work — tick the box above, or email onlygoodantibodies@gmail.com.
  • Modification-specific antibodies are out of scope. Our verdicts rest on knockout controls, and a knockout removes the whole protein — so it cannot show whether an antibody is specific for a phosphorylated or otherwise modified form.
