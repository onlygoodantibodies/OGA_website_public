Our Platform
What we offer
OGA brings together a searchable database of knockout-validated antibody characterisation data, a free e-learning Academy, an Antibody Champions scheme, and an evidence-based roadmap for stakeholder action. Everything is free and open access.
🔍

Antibody Database

Search knockout-validated characterisation data across western blot, immunoprecipitation, immunofluorescence, and flow cytometry. Compare antibodies from 14 manufacturers side by side, with application-specific recommendations. All data generated through our collaboration with YCharOS Inc.

🎓

OGA Academy

Free, self-paced e-learning covering antibody selection, the five pillars of validation, database navigation, and data interpretation. Five modules with quizzes and a downloadable PDF certificate on completion.

🏅

Antibody Champions

A joint OGA/NC3Rs programme recruiting early career researchers to drive culture change in antibody validation at their own institutions. 12-month programme with professional training from Inspire Change and expert mentorship from AstraZeneca and YCharOS.

📄

Evidence & Roadmap

Two peer-reviewed preprints published March 2026 — quantifying the ethical and scientific costs of inadequate validation and presenting 15 consensus recommendations from 32 international experts, with action plans for institutions, funders, publishers, and manufacturers.

Open Science Partnership
Our collaboration with YCharOS Inc.
The OGA community and YCharOS Inc. work together to increase the availability and use of well-performing research antibodies, eliminate the use of inappropriate antibodies and the associated waste of animals for their production, and promote education and awareness about best practices in antibody use and validation. Our YCharOS collaborative lab has been running since 2021 and now spans four laboratories, characterising antibodies from 14 manufacturers using standardised knockout-validated protocols.
Our Projects
Three projects
Our work runs in three strands.

Better Antibodies

Identifying robust reagents to study key targets of interest, in collaboration with antibody manufacturers through YCharOS and the NC3Rs. Characterising antibodies across four applications with knockout-validated controls to build a trusted, open dataset.

Funded by NC3Rs

Learn more →

Better Science

Driving better standards in antibody choice and use across the research lifecycle — from funding applications through purchasing decisions, experimental design, and manuscript assessment. Developing the database, training resources, and stakeholder engagement tools.

Funded by MRC-NIHR Better Methods, Better Research

Learn more →

Better Patient Outcomes

Using well-characterised antibodies to identify proteins that predict treatment responses in interstitial lung disease (ILD) patients — working towards better diagnosis and more targeted treatment selection through robust, reproducible antibody tools.

Supported by Leicester Institute for Precision Health & MRC Impact Accelerator

Affiliations & Acknowledgements

OGA is led from the University of Leicester and is an affiliate stakeholder of the UK Reproducibility Network. Our work is supported by the NC3Rs, the MRC-NIHR Better Methods Better Research programme, the Leicester Institute for Precision Health, and the NIHR Leicester Biomedical Research Centre. We are part of the wider movement to improve research integrity and reproducibility, and our work represents an opportunity for learning in the wider context of trustworthy research and reliable diagnostics.

Open Source

The code that runs this site is public, at github.com/onlygoodantibodies/OGA_website_public. It is released under the Apache License 2.0, and the characterisation data that ships alongside it under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 — you may read it, run it, and build on it, including commercially.
