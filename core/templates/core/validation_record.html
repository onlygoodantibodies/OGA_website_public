Antibody Validation Record

onlygoodantibodies.co.uk/tools/validation-record

Use this to document antibody validation for a manuscript, a grant, or your own records. It follows the OGA Validation Planning Framework: for each antibody, say what it was, what it was used for, and which controls you ran and why. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is sent to us. When you're done, use Download / Print to save a PDF or Copy for pasting into a document.
