Community & Collaboration

Our Partners

The OGA community brings together antibody manufacturers, research funders, publishers, reagent databases, and research institutions. Partners contribute in several distinct ways, and the labels on each card below show exactly how. Some supply antibody reagents to our Leicester YCharOS laboratory; some lent their expertise to our Delphi consensus study; some fund the work or sponsor the Antibody Champions scheme; and some help disseminate characterisation data across the research ecosystem. We are grateful to all of them.

How our partners contribute
Reagents Supplied antibodies to the Leicester YCharOS lab
Delphi Expert participation in the consensus study
Funding Grants, studentships, or co-investment
Champions Supports the Antibody Champions scheme
Data Helps disseminate antibody characterisation data
Host Hosts the OGA platform and team
Our Consortium
YCharOS

YCharOS is an open-science antibody characterisation consortium that develops expert consensus protocols and tests reagents against knockout controls. Only Good Antibodies operates the consortium's UK characterisation laboratory at the University of Leicester, generating the knockout-controlled characterisation data at the heart of the OGA database.
