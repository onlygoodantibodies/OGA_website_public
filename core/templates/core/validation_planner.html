Before you start — the idea in one minute
Validation should be proportionate to the conclusions your antibody carries. You don't need to validate every antibody to the same depth — you need to match the evidence to what the antibody is being asked to prove.
  • Search first. Existing characterisation data (e.g. OGA / YCharOS) can tell you which antibodies already work — before you spend on validation.
  • The strongest negative control is genetic — a knockout or knockdown, ideally in the same cell line and application as your experiment.
  • Corroborate antibody results with an antibody-independent readout (mRNA, mass spec, orthogonal antibody) where you can.
