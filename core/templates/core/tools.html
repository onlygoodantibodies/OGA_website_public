Antibody Selection Tool

Answer a few questions about your target and application and get a specific plan: which antibody to choose, the knockout-controlled validation that proves it, and the controls to run — grounded in independent YCharOS characterisation data. All the tools are free. What happens to what you type differs:

  • Validation Record — stays in your browser; we receive nothing.
  • Selection Tool — a plan you save is kept by OGA, and a compliance record may be shared with the funder who asked you to use it.

Gene symbols you enter are looked up against UniProt and public expression datasets. The Academy and the AI connector are covered in our Privacy Policy. Built on recommendations from a multi-stakeholder Delphi consensus study.
