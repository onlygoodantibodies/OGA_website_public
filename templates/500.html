Django renders a 500 with an EMPTY context and no context processors — by design, because whatever broke may be exactly what a context processor would touch. So there is no `request` here, nothing dynamic, and no `{% templatetag openblock %} static {% templatetag closeblock %}`: `staticfiles` needs its storage backend, which is one of the things that can be broken when this page is what the reader gets. Plain paths and inline styles only. What it must say is the thing a scientist actually needs to know: the failure was in showing a page, not in the data. Every write path in this app previews before it commits, so a 500 during a save means nothing was saved — a much better message than a stack trace, and the one the default page cannot give. {% endcomment %} Something went wrong – Only Good Antibodies
