Somebody searched the public site, could not find their gene, and told us they need it. Most-asked first, with the applications they said they need.

{% comment %} Said before the table, because the two lists look alike and adding them together is the mistake this page invites. A request is demand from outside with nobody paying for it; a nomination is a site, a funder and a bench. The Portfolio counts the second and must never count the first. {% endcomment %}

These are not nominations. Nothing here is on anybody's list, funded, or committed to — it is what people outside the consortium have asked for. A request becomes work by somebody deciding it should: check it on Look a gene up and add it there, or add it straight from the target board. That write is what creates a nomination; this page never does.
