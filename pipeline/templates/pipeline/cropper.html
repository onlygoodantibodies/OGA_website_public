This press no longer replaces anything on the public site — the crops wait in the review queue — so the acknowledgement is now about what *release* will do: a crop for an antibody+application that already has a published figure is queued as a replacement for it. The person who made the crop is the one who knows whether that is intended, and they are standing here rather than at the review meeting.{% endcomment %}
3 · Antibodies
Parse fills the catalogue list + the editable metadata grid (right, under the crops). Blanks are fine; catalogues drive OCR mapping and filenames.
4 · Seed grid
Then drag to fit. Orange = edges (top/bottom span the figure; each row's left & right are independent, so an inset/centred row brackets just its panels). Blue = row dividers. Green = column splits (double-click a row to add one, Alt-click any green/blue line to delete). A row of N panels needs N−1 green lines between its two orange edges.
5 · Assign
Click a cell to assign/discard. Assigned cells are numbered in reading order. Then run OCR to read the printed titles and pre-fill the mapping (falls back to reading order for unclear reads).
Multi-row tip: put each row's blue divider above that row's titles, so every panel's title sits inside its own cell — that's what OCR reads (and what the crop keeps).
