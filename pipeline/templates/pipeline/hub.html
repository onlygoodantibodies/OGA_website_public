Sorted by role, the three boards led and Check feasibility — step one of everything — sat fifth, so the annotated walkthrough had to draw a box round it captioned "1 · Everything starts here". Frequency ordering serves the person who already knows the app; a hub is for the one who does not. The "your usual" chips above are still role-aware, so both readers are served — one control each, instead of two fighting over one.
