Key message: the same results are available where you need them, in the paper you are reading, the antibody you are choosing, and your own systems.

All of these are free and listed under Tools in the site's menu.

  • Browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox). Open a paper on a journal site, a preprint server or Europe PMC, and every antibody YCharOS has characterised is marked in the text, coloured by its result in the application that paper used it for. Hover to see the figure. It can also mark supplier catalogue pages while you shop, and it flags antibodies reported to recognise the wrong target. Matching happens in your browser; no page text is sent anywhere.
  • Check a paper's antibodies with your own AI (a prototype). Connect OGA to Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant that supports MCP connectors, and ask it to check a manuscript. It looks up each antibody the paper cites and reports the per-application result. Ask, and it also reports which specificity controls the paper itself shows for each one: demonstrated, present but not linked to that antibody, or absent.
  • Antibody Selection Tool. Answer a few questions about your target and application and get a plan: which antibody to choose, the knockout-controlled evidence behind it and the controls to run, as a downloadable PDF.
  • Validation Planning Framework and Validation Record. The framework sets out the principles of proportionate validation. The record is a quick form for documenting what you did and why, and it stays in your browser.
  • Data Portal & API. For antibody manufacturers, registries and data partners: browse and download the results and figures, or keep a catalogue in step with OGA's automatically. Access is by key.
  • Antibody Champions. A community of researchers, run with the NC3Rs, who support good antibody practice in their own institutions.

Each tool reports the same four results you learned in this module, because they all draw on the same data.
