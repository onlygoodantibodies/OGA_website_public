Key message: one search box takes a gene, a catalogue number or an RRID, and it is on every page.

The large box on the homepage and the small one at the top of every other page behave the same way. Type any of these:

  • A gene symbol, e.g. SOD1. Other names work too: typing an alias such as ALS1 offers SOD1, with the alias shown beside it.
  • A catalogue number, e.g. 10269-1-AP. It does not matter how the paper or supplier printed it: with or without hyphens, a leading # or a thousands comma, it is still found.
  • An RRID, e.g. AB_2193750.

Suggestions appear as you type: genes straight away, antibodies a moment later, each with its supplier and target. Pick an antibody and you land on its gene page with that antibody highlighted, even if it is further down the page or the supplier no longer sells it.

Prefer to look around? Browse every gene, under the search box, lists every gene with public data, with its other names and how many antibodies were tested. Next to it, Or by funder lists the genes a particular funder paid for.

The homepage also shows how many genes, antibodies and tests are in the database today. These numbers grow each time a new report is released, which is why this module does not quote them.
