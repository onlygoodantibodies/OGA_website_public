Key message: cite the antibody by its RRID and the data by its report, so that others can find exactly what you used.

  • Cite each antibody with its RRID in your methods, in the form Proteintech Cat# 10269-1-AP, RRID:AB_2193750. A catalogue number can change or be reused, but an RRID always points to the same reagent.
  • Cite the report when you use OGA's results or figures. Copy Citation on the gene page gives you the reference, and the CC BY 4.0 licence lets you reuse a figure as long as you do.
  • Check the supplier's page before buying. Products are withdrawn and renamed. The supplier link on each antibody takes you to the current datasheet.
  • Plan your own controls. A supportive result narrows your choice. It does not replace a specificity control in your own system. The Selection Tool and the Validation Planning Framework help you decide which control you need.
