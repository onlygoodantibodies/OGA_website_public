Curriculum
What you'll learn
Four taught modules take you from the fundamentals of antibody selection through to interpreting real validation data. Each includes a quiz — pass all four to unlock the final assessment and earn your certificate.
Module 1

Why Antibody Choice Matters

Understand why antibody selection affects experimental reproducibility, resource use, and ethical commitments. Learn the differences between polyclonal, monoclonal, and recombinant antibodies and what makes an antibody suitable for research.

12–15 minutes · 6 sections · quiz
Module 2

The Five Pillars of Antibody Validation

Explore the five complementary validation strategies: genetic (knockout/knockdown), orthogonal, independent antibody, tagged expression, and immunocapture mass spectrometry. Learn when to apply each approach.

12–15 minutes · 7 sections · quiz
Module 3

Navigating the OGA Database

Learn to search for antibodies by gene name, interpret antibody detail pages including RRID, host, clone ID, and recommended applications, and compare antibodies using the OGA database's validation data.

10–12 minutes · 5 sections · quiz
Module 4

Interpreting Validation Data

Develop practical skills in reading flow cytometry histograms, evaluating western blots, and critically assessing overexpression data. Learn to recognise common pitfalls and apply converging evidence when genetic controls are unavailable.

10–15 minutes · 7 sections · quiz
Final Assessment

Comprehensive Quiz & Certification

Test your knowledge across all four modules. Score 80% or above to earn your OGA Academy certificate — a downloadable PDF you can share with supervisors, include in grant applications, or add to your CV.

Cumulative assessment · 80% pass mark · PDF certificate
